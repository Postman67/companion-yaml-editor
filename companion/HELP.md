## Button YAML Editor

This connection serves a text editor for your buttons. Open it at `/instance/<connection label>/`
on your Companion web address (e.g. `http://127.0.0.1:8000/instance/yaml_editor/`).

The editor is currently **read-only**: pick a page and a button to see its full configuration as YAML,
updated live as the button changes.

It has no actions, feedbacks or variables of its own.
